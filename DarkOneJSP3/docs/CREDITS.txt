DarkOneJSP3 Credits and Attribution
===================================

DarkOneJSP3 is an unofficial community continuation of the DarkOne family of
foobar2000 themes. It combines the original DarkOne visual identity and panel
concepts with a modern x64 architecture based on Columns UI, JSplitter 4.x and
JScript Panel 3.8.5.

Project direction and adaptation
--------------------------------

- DarkOne2021 adaptation and maintenance.
- DarkOneJSP3 project direction, design decisions, migration requirements,
  integration, testing and quality assurance.
- Modern x64 layout, configuration and usability enhancements.
